ColorMasumi family palette

One palette for Masumi, Sokosumi and CMO.XYZ. It runs from pink through red and yellow, with softer tones taken from traditional Japanese colors.

Iris Flower is the primary and the single accent. Every other color is secondary.

Text on Iris Flower is always Neutral 950, never white.
